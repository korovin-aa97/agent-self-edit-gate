# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for
different viewpoints, accountability, and focus on what is best for the
community. Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, sexualized attention,
trolling, insults, threats, publishing private information, or other conduct
that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and
other contributions that violate this Code, and may temporarily or permanently
ban contributors. Report abuse privately through the repository's GitHub
Security reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept
confidential as far as practical.
